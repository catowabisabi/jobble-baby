Implement Neurodevelopmental Milestone Notifier — brain-building schedule based on baby's age, weekly focus areas, daily micro-prompts, and critical period closing alerts. idea #18.

## Requirements
1. New tab or expand existing milestones.tsx: "Brain Builder" section
2. Uses baby.birthDate from AsyncStorage @jobble_baby_profile to compute current age in months
3. WHO/AAP developmental windows mapped to ages:
   - 0-2mo: visual tracking, face recognition
   - 2-4mo: reaching, grasping, cooing
   - 4-6mo: rolling, sitting with support, babbling
   - 6-9mo: sitting, crawling, pincer grasp
   - 9-12mo: pulling to stand, first words, object permanence
   - 12-18mo: walking, vocabulary explosion, parallel play
4. Weekly focus: show current week's developmental goal (single focus area)
5. Daily micro-prompt: one targeted stimulation activity suggestion (e.g. "Tummy time: 3x5min today with mirror toy")
6. Critical period alerts: warn when current window is in last 2 weeks (e.g. "Crawling window closes in 10 days!")
7. Reuses existing MILESTONE_TYPES from milestones.tsx
8. Badge: "Brain Builder" badge when 5 daily prompts completed in a week
