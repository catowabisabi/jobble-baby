- Full-featured vestibular-motor integration tab with 7 spec sections:
  - **Vestibular Activity Logger**: Log rocking (vertical/horizontal/side-to-side), bouncing, swinging, active tummy-time with duration and intensity (low/medium/high)
  - **Motor Milestone Correlation**: Bridge showing vestibular activity frequency vs motor milestones (head control, rolling, sitting, crawling)
  - **Sleep-Vestibular Correlation**: Bar chart showing sleep quality (1-5) after logged vestibular activities
  - **Gut Motility Connection**: Icons showing feeding outcomes (normal, spitting up, constipation, gas) with counts

  - **Optimal Vestibular Window Alerts**: Banner shown when baby is 4-8 months old
