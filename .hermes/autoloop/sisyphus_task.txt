## Full Implementation Details

### State
- entries array: { id, date, week_number, fontanelle_size_mm, photo_uri, head_circumference_cm, notes, alert_triggered }
- currentWeek number, isAddingEntry boolean, showAlert boolean

### UI Components
1. Header: "Fontanelle Soft Spot Map" (en) / "臍門追蹤" (zh)
2. Greeting: "Track your baby's soft spot size and head growth"
3. Alert Banner: red if fontanelle size increases unexpectedly (possible dehydration) or closes before 9 months
4. Weekly Entry Card: current week number, fontanelle size input (slider 0-60mm), head circumference input (cm)
5. Photo Capture: optional photo of fontanelle area (camera or gallery)
6. Growth Correlation: simple chart overlaying fontanelle size on head circumference growth
7. Closure Timeline: visual progress toward closure (expected 12-18 months)
8. History: FlatList of past entries
9. Milestone badge: "Soft Spot Guardian" when 4+ weeks logged

### Measurement Guidance
- Anterior fontanelle: measure in mm, typically 30-50mm at birth
- Normal closure range: 12-18 months
- Alert conditions:
  - Size increases >5mm vs prior week (possible dehydration)
  - Closure occurs before 9 months (early closure)
  - Head circumference drops below 3rd percentile
