## Task: Implement Home Safety Audit Tab

Create a tab for parents to systematically audit their home for baby hazards — room by room.

## Requirements

### Tab Name
`home-safety` — add to `JobbleBaby/app/(tabs)/` and register in `_layout.tsx`

### Features
1. **Room-by-Room Audit Checklist**
   - Rooms: Kitchen, Bathroom, Nursery, Living Room, Stairs, Outdoors
   - Categories per room: Fall hazards, Burn hazards, Choking hazards, Poison hazards, Drowning hazards, Strangulation hazards
   - Toggle: Safe / Unsafe / Not Applicable per item
   - Count of unsafe items per room

2. **Safety Score**
   - Overall home safety score (0-100%) based on completed items
   - Score per room breakdown
   - Color-coded: red <50%, amber 50-80%, green >80%

3. **Priority Alerts**
   - List of unsafe items sorted by severity (high/medium/low)
   - High-priority items highlighted
   - "Action required" count badge

4. **Babyproofing Checklist with Action Tips**
   - Specific actionable tips for each unsafe item
   - e.g., "Outlet covers not installed" → "Insert tamper-resistant outlet covers on all accessible outlets"
   - e.g., "Cabinet locks missing" → "Install child-resistant cabinet locks on lower cabinets"

5. **Audit Progress Tracker**
   - Rooms completed vs total
   - Last audit date
   - Re-audit reminder (30/60/90 day options)

6. **Correlation Features**
   - Link to teething tab (baby exploring with mouth → choking hazard awareness)
   - Link to sleep-training tab (crib safety)
   - Link to growth tab (developmental stage affects hazard severity)

7. **i18n**
   - Add keys under `homeSafety.*` in en.json + zh.json
   - All user-facing strings must use i18n
