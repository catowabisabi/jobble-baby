### Features

1. **Stage Selector**
   - 3 stages displayed as cards:
     - Stage 1: Soft Spout (6-7 months)
     - Stage 2: Straw Cup (7-9 months)
     - Stage 3: Open Cup (9-12 months)
   - Visual progress indicator showing current stage
   - Tap to select and view stage details

2. **Practice Session Logger**
   - Log each practice: date, duration (minutes), cup type, sip success rate (1-5), spill level (1-5), notes
   - Save to AsyncStorage key: `@jobble/cup_feeding_entries`
   - Show practice history list

3. **Hydration Score Tracker**
   - Track fluid intake by source: Bottle, Breast, Cup
   - Simple bar/section showing daily intake by source
   - Total daily fluid goal indicator

4. **Milestones Tracker**
   - "First open cup success" milestone
   - "Straw cup mastered (14 consecutive days)" milestone
   - Visual badge display for achieved milestones

5. **Parent Tips Section**
   - "Avoid prolonged bottle/sippy cup use to prevent tooth decay"
   - "Correlate cup timing with bowel movement patterns" (gastrocolic reflex tip)
   - Display tips with an info icon
