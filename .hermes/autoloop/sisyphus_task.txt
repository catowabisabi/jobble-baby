## Task: Implement Baby Safety Audit Navigator tab

Create app/(tabs)/safety-audit.tsx — guided home safety checklist.

### Features
1. **Sleep Environment Check**
   - Crib slat spacing less than 2.375 inches
   - No crib bumpers, pillow, or soft bedding
   - Firm mattress, fitted sheet only
   - Baby alone on back
   - Checkbox for each item plus pass or fail toggle

2. **Car Seat Safety**
   - Rear-facing confirmation
   - Harness slot at or below shoulder level
   - Chest clip at armpit level
   - Car seat expiration date check
   - Photo capture option for each zone

3. **Babywearing Safety**
   - M-position hips (frog legs)
   - Airway visible (chin off chest)
   - No forward-facing before 6 months
   - No unsupervised car in carrier

4. **Bath Safety**
   - Max 2 inches water depth
   - Elbow test confirmation (water temp below 100F or 38C)
   - No ring toys that can trap fingers
   - Never leave unattended

5. **Feeding Safety**
   - No honey before 12 months
   - Upright positioning during feeds
   - Paced bottle feeding to prevent aspiration
   - Proper head elevation

6. **Supervision Zones**
   - Fall prevention: elevated surfaces, stairs
   - Stair gates installed top and bottom
   - No unattended baby on changing table
   - Hot surface check

### Data
- AsyncStorage key: @jobble/safety_audit_entries
- Schema: zone (string), items array ({ label, passed boolean, photo optional }), date (string), notes (string)
- Each audit is one entry, newest first
- Exportable summary for pediatrician or landlord
