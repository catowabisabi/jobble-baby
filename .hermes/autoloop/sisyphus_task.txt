### Features

1. **Bilirubin Level Input**
   - Input field for total serum bilirubin (TSB) in mg/dL or μmol/L
   - Unit toggle: mg/dL ↔ μmol/L (1 mg/dL = 17.1 μmol/L)
   - Date/time stamp for each entry
   - Age in hours input (calculate from baby birthdate if available from `@jobble_baby_profile`)

2. **Risk Zone Chart**
   - Simple SVG line chart showing bilirubin vs age-in-hours
   - X-axis: baby's age in hours (0–336h / 14 days)
   - Y-axis: bilirubin level in mg/dL
   - 3–4 zone bands: Low Risk (green), Monitor (yellow), High Risk (orange), Exchange (red)
   - Use `react-native-svg` for the chart (already available)
   - Plot the current reading on the chart if age is provided

3. **Risk Zone Reference**
   - Display Bhutani nomogram-based thresholds for:
     - Low risk: <8 mg/dL (breastfed) / <10 mg/dL (formula)
     - Monitor: 8–12 / 10–15 mg/dL
     - High risk: 12–15 / 15–18 mg/dL
     - Exchange transfusion: >15 / >18 mg/dL
   - Disclaimer: "Consult your pediatrician for medical decisions"

4. **History Log**
   - List of past readings with timestamp, level, zone, and trend arrow
   - Trend: compare to previous reading (↑ rising, ↓ falling, → stable within 1 mg/dL)

5. **Alert System**
   - If bilirubin enters High Risk or Exchange zone, show Alert.alert
   - Link to existing jaundice tab for phototherapy guidance
