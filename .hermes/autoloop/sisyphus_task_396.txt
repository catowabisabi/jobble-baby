## Upgrade Requirements

### 1. New Section: Air Travel Ear Comfort Protocol
- Pre-flight readiness checklist:
  - Baby's age (≥7 days for most airlines, some require ≥14 days)
  - Current congestion level (none/mild/moderate/severe)
  - Recent ear infection (yes/no/unknown)
  - Last feeding time
- Pressure equalization logging:
  - Flight date, flight number
  - Descent start time, descent end time
  - Baby distress level during descent (0-10)

- Post-flight summary

- Add timeline view of stranger anxiety progression
