## Full spec

### Problem
The existing Polyvagal Dashboard (parent) and Autonomic Readiness Index (baby) are two separate tools. Parents miss the real-time coupling between their own nervous system state and their baby's physiological regulation. Coregulation is not just about behavior — it's about neural coupling: when a parent's ventral vagal system is activated (calm, socially engaged), their baby's autonomic stability measurably improves within minutes. No app tracks this bidirectional resonance loop.

### Solution
A unified navigator that tracks parent-baby autonomic coupling in real-time, identifies resonance patterns, and guides parents to intentionally use their own regulatory capacity to soothe and stabilize their baby.

### Sections to implement

1. **Resonance Logger** — Parent logs their own Polyvagal state (ventral vagal/sympathetic/dorsal) alongside a simultaneous baby state indicator (calm/aroused/distressed). App timestamps each paired entry.

2. **Coupling Strength Meter** — After 7+ paired entries, calculates a "Resonance Score" — how often baby's state improves within 5 minutes of parent entering ventral vagal. Shows as a % coupling strength.

3. **Vagal Brake Prompt** — When baby is distressed, app generates a specific "vagal brake" stimulus:
   - Slow, exhalation-focused breathing cue
   - Soft cooing at 250-400Hz prompt
   - Gentle vestibular stimulation (rocking pattern)
   - Contingent responsiveness prompt (name baby's state, narrate calm)

4. **Resonance Timeline** — Line chart: parent polyvagal state vs. baby state over past 14 days. Color bands show coupling windows.

5. **Allostatic Load Dashboard** — Tracks cumulative stress load for both parent and baby: sleep debt, illness burden, feeding stress, emotional dysregulation. Color-coded gauge (green=low, red=high).

6. **Resonance Alert** — Flags: parent chronically in dorsal/sympathetic (compassion fatigue risk), baby not responding to parent's ventral vagal state (referral indicator).
