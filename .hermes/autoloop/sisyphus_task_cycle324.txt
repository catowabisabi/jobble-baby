Write the concept spec for "Interoceptive Precision Training" (concept #52 in DB).

Keywords: interoception, interoceptive_accuracy, body_awareness

Background: The parent-baby dyad shares an interoceptive ecosystem. Interoceptive precision — the ability to perceive and act on internal bodily signals — develops from birth and is critical for emotion regulation, hunger/satiety awareness, and pain tolerance. A parent with high interoceptive precision can detect baby distress cues earlier. A baby with developing interoceptive precision learns to self-regulate.

Research basis:
- Interoceptive accuracy declines under stress (cortisol overload)
- Polyvagal theory: vagal tone is measurable via HRV and correlates with interoceptive precision
- Infants are born with coarse interoception but refine it through caregiver co-regulation
- Gut-brain axis awareness is a missing construct in baby apps
