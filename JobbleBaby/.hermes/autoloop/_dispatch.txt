## Requirements

Create `app/(tabs)/feeding-readiness.tsx` — a tab to help parents know when baby is ready for solids, what flavors to introduce, and track the complementary feeding journey.

### Features
1. **Feeding Readiness Checklist** — 6 signs baby is ready: head control, sitting support, tongue thrust regression, food interest, doubled birth weight, pincer grasp preview. Each sign has a visual guide and checkbox.
2. **Flavor Journey Journal** — log first tastes across categories: bland, umami, sweet, sour. 12-week flavor map with color coding (green=accepted, yellow=reluctant, red=rejected). Track food items with date, amount, reaction severity (1-3).
3. **Texture Progression Tracker** — 4 stages: purees (4-6m) → mashable (6-8m) → finger foods (8-10m) → family meals (10-12m). Visual timeline showing current stage and transition markers.
4. **WHO Allergen Introduction Log** — guided schedule for top 9 allergens (peanut, egg, milk, soy, wheat, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, sesame). Log introduction date and reaction severity (none/mild/moderate/severe).
5. **Growth Correlation View** — overlay feeding frequency with weight/height percentile from growth.tsx. Show correlation trend line.
6. **Iron-Rich Food Reminders** — at 6m+, suggest iron-rich foods (fortified cereal, pureed meats, beans). Track intake vs WHO recommended.
7. **Feeding Alerts** — prompt for: "Start solids at 6 months", "Introduce allergens one at a time", "Iron-rich foods needed at 6m"
8. **Badge**: "Flavor Explorer" — logged 20+ distinct foods in first year

### Data Model
- AsyncStorage: `@jobble/feeding_readiness` — { checklist_done: boolean, date: string }
- AsyncStorage: `@jobble/flavor_journal` — [{ id, date, category, food, amount_g, reaction_severity }]
- AsyncStorage: `@jobble/texture_stage` — { current_stage: 1-4, transition_dates: [] }
- AsyncStorage: `@jobble/allergen_log` — [{ allergen, date_introduced, reaction_severity }]
