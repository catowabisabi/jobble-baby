Implement Concept #27: Colic Relief & Purple Crying Navigator.

## Task
Create `app/(tabs)/colic-relief.tsx` — a new tab to help parents navigate the Period of PURPLE Crying and colic.

## Requirements
1. **Crying Pattern Tracker** — log fussy episodes with duration, intensity (1-10), triggers (dropdown: hunger, wind, overtired, overstimulated, reflux, teething, unknown)
2. **5 S's Activation Guide** — step-by-step Swaddle, Side-hold, Shush, Swing, Suck techniques with visual guide cards. Each S has an illustrated instruction card.
3. **Colic Comfort Arsenal** — action cards: bicycle legs, belly massage, warm compress, gripe water, probiotic drops — each tappable to log usage + duration
4. **Purple Crying Education** — explain the normalcy of excessive crying (peaking at 2 months), when to seek medical help (intussusception signs, hernia)
5. **Real-time Crying Timer** — start/stop timer, track total crying hours per day, show 7-day trend bar chart
6. **White Noise Library** — 4 built-in sounds (vacuum, fan, shusher, ocean) with play/stop + timer (15/30/60 min)
7. **Caregiver Stress Monitor** — if crying exceeds 5 hours/day, show bright alert for caregiver break + emergency contacts
8. **Badge**: "Survived the Purple" — earned after logging 7 consecutive days of colic management
