## 1. First Launch — Baby Profile Setup

On first open, Jobble Baby shows an onboarding screen. Fill in:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Baby's Name** | Free text — used throughout the app for personalised greetings |
| **Birth Date** | Date picker — used to calculate corrected age (for preemies) and milestone expectations |
| **Gender** | Boy / Girl / Prefer not to say — used for gender-specific growth chart references |

Tap **Get Started**. This data is saved to your device's local storage and never leaves your phone.

### Editing the Profile Later
1. Go to the **Profile** tab (last tab, right side)
2. Tap any field to edit
3. Changes save automatically

### Resetting the Profile
Profile → Settings → Reset Profile → Confirm. This clears all baby data and returns you to the onboarding screen.

## 3. Growth Charts — WHO Percentile Explained

### What the Chart Shows
The Growth tab plots your baby's weight, length, and head circumference on WHO growth standard charts.

- **X-axis**: Baby's age (0–24 months)
- **Y-axis**: Measurement (kg / cm / inch)
- **Coloured lines**: WHO percentile curves (3rd, 15th, 50th, 85th, 97th)

### How to Read Percentiles
> The 50th percentile does **not** mean "average" or "target." It is the midpoint — half of healthy babies are above, half below.

| Percentile | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 3rd–97th | Normal range — most healthy babies fall here |
| Below 3rd | Consult doctor — may indicate slow growth |
| Above 97th | Consult doctor — may indicate rapid growth |
| Crossing lines sharply | Discuss with pediatrician (e.g., dropping from 75th to 15th) |

### Adding a Measurement
1. Go to **Growth** tab
2. Tap **+ Add Measurement**
3. Enter date, weight, length, head circumference
4. Chart updates automatically

### Corrected Age (Premature Babies)
If your baby was born early, Jobble Baby can display **corrected age** (subtracts weeks early from current age). Profile → toggle "Use corrected age." Milestone expectations adjust accordingly.

## 4. Sleep Training Methods

Jobble Baby includes four evidence-based sleep training methods. Choose the one that fits your family's philosophy and comfort level.

### Method 1 — Ferber / Graduated Extinction
- Put baby down drowsy but awake
- If crying, wait 3 min (first night), 5 min (second), 10 min (subsequent)
- Go in briefly to pat/restore, but do not pick up
- Repeat with increasing intervals until baby self-soothes
- **Best for**: Babies 5–6 months+ with no health concerns

### Method 2 — Camping Out
- Sit beside the cot until baby falls asleep
- Over successive nights, move your chair farther away
- Eventually you are outside the room
- **Best for**: Parents uncomfortable with any crying

### Method 3 — Full Extinction (Cry It Out)
- Put baby down awake
- Do not return until a pre-set time (e.g., 6 hours or morning)
- **Best for**: Parents who can tolerate short-term intense crying for faster results

### Method 4 — Chair Method
- Sit in a chair next to the cot
- Each night, move the chair farther away
- Eventually out of the room
- **Best for**: Gentle, slow approach with parental presence

### Before Starting Sleep Training
1. Baby is healthy and gaining weight well
2. You and your partner are aligned on the method
3. No major life changes (travel, moving) in the next 2 weeks
4. Consult your pediatrician if baby has reflux, failure to thrive, or other health concerns

## 5. Doctor Visit Summary Export

Before every pediatrician visit, use Doctor Visit to prepare and after the visit to record and export a summary.

### Pre-Visit Checklist
1. Go to **Doctor Visit** tab
2. Review the checklist — bring any requested items (vaccine record, growth chart printout)
3. Note down questions you want to ask

### Exporting a Visit Summary
1. Doctor Visit → Enter visit details (date, doctor name, reason)
2. Tap **Export Summary**
3. Choose format: PDF or plain text
4. Share via email, AirDrop, or save to Files
5. The summary includes:
   - Growth history (all measurements plotted)
   - Milestone status (which milestones passed/not yet)
   - Recent notes from Tracking tab
   - Any allergen exposures logged

## 6. Data Storage & Privacy

### Where Is My Data?
**All data is stored locally** on your phone using AsyncStorage (iOS) / SharedPreferences (Android). There is:
- ❌ No cloud sync
- ❌ No user account required
- ❌ No data sent to any server
- ✅ 100% private

### Exporting Your Data
Profile → Export Data → choose JSON backup file. This downloads a complete JSON snapshot of all your data including:
- Baby profile
- All tracking entries (feeds, sleep, diapers)
- Growth measurements
- Milestone completions
- All screen settings

### Importing Data (New Phone)
Profile → Import Data → select the JSON backup file. All data is restored to the new device.

## 7. Getting Help & Support

### In-App Help
- Most screens have a **?** icon in the top-right corner — tap for contextual help
- **Profile → About** shows version and links

### Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Baby profile disappeared | Profile → enter details again (data may still be in AsyncStorage — force-quit and reopen app first) |
| Growth chart not updating | Add a new measurement via + button, then pull-to-refresh the chart |
| Sleep training log missing | Sleep Training tab → scroll down to Session Log |
| Language switch not working | Profile → Language → select English or 中文 → restart app |
| Export file is empty | Try again — if it persists, use Profile → Export Data (JSON) which is always complete |

### Contact Support
For bugs, feature requests, or questions:
- Email: **support@jobblebaby.com** *(placeholder — configure before launch)*
- GitHub Issues: **github.com/jobblebaby/app/issues**

### Submitting a Doctor Visit Summary via Email

2. Tap Share → Mail
3. Address to your pediatrician
4. Send
