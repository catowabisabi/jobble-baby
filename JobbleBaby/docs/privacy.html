Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 23, 2026

This policy is published at jobblebaby.com/privacy

Data Collection

What Data We Collect

Jobble Baby collects the following data you manually enter about your baby:

  • Feeding logs (breastfeeding, bottle feeding, solid foods)
  • Sleep logs (sleep times, duration, naps)
  • Diaper change logs
  • Growth records (height, weight, head circumference)
  • Milestones and developmental notes
  • Health information (temperature, medications, vaccinations)

How We Collect Data

All data is entered manually by you, the parent or guardian. Jobble Baby does NOT collect any data from your device's sensors (such as microphone, camera, or location).

Important: Data is collected only when you voluntarily enter it into the app. We do not automatically collect any information.

Data Transmission

Your data is NEVER transmitted to any server. All information stays on your device. Jobble Baby operates completely offline — an internet connection is not required for any core features.

Data Storage

Local Storage Only

All data you enter is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage, a secure local storage system. This means:

  • Your baby's information never leaves your device
  • Data persists between app sessions
  • Data remains on your device until you delete the app or explicitly clear the data

Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. If you delete the app or clear its data, all stored information will be permanently removed.

Backup

By default, Jobble Baby does NOT automatically back up your data to any cloud service. Any cloud backup you configure through your device (such as iCloud or Google Drive) is controlled by your device settings, not by Jobble Baby.

Data Sharing

Jobble Baby has a strict policy of NOT sharing any data:

  • NO data is shared with third parties
  • NO data is sold to any party
  • NO analytics or tracking services are integrated
  • NO advertising identifiers are collected
  • NO data is shared with our own servers (because we don't have any)
We do not share anonymized or aggregated data because we share no data at all, period.

This Privacy Policy reflects our actual practices. There are no hidden data collection mechanisms in Jobble Baby.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Jobble Baby is designed for parents and guardians to track information about their children. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • Jobble Baby does NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • The user (parent/guardian) is the one entering data about their child — not the child themselves
  • All data belongs to the family unit and is stored on the family's device
  • We do not allow children to create accounts or submit their own information

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at support@jobblebaby.com.
