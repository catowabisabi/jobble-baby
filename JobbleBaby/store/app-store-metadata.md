**MEET JOBBLE BABY**
The privacy-first baby companion for HK and Asian parents. "Fewer clicks, more presence." Jobble Baby reduces parental cognitive load through ambient, glanceable tracking — not dashboards or gamification overload.

**CORE TRACKING — ONE TAP**
• Sleep tracker: nap cycles, nighttime sleep, wake windows
• Feeding log: breast, bottle, solids — duration, amount, side
• Diaper log: wet/dry/soiled with timestamp and notes
• Growth chart: WHO percentile for height, weight, head circumference

**ADVANCED FEATURES**
• Milestone tracker with photo capture + age stamp
• FPIES/IgE/non-IgE allergen reaction timeline
• Sleep training curriculum tracker (Ferber/extinction/chair/fading)
• Context-aware outing checklist (weather + trip type)
• Circadian dashboard + shift-handoff for caregivers
• Parental burnout detection + breathing intervention
• 20-tooth chart + teething pain/remedy log
• Milk prep guide: thaw guidelines, freezer stash, expiry tracking

**GEAR + SCHEDULING**
• Doctor visit export — shareable pediatrician summary
• QR code caregiver schedule sharing
• Primitive reflex milestone tracker
• Sleep debt dashboard — parental deficit vs baby regression correlation
• Emergency SOS: panic mode with breathing + safe space checklist

**WHY PARENTS LOVE JOBBLE BABY**
• Calm technology: no alarms, no badges, no dark patterns
• Dark theme default + light mode (WCAG 2.1 AA)
• One-hand operation for sleep-deprived parents
• All data on-device — no account, no cloud, no data selling
• Supports English and Traditional Chinese

**PRIVACY FIRST**
All data stays on your device. No account required. No analytics. No ads. Export/import JSON backup anytime.
