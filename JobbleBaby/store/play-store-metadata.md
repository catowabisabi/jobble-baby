## Full Description (4000 chars)

**MEET JOBBLE BABY**
The privacy-first baby companion for HK and Asian parents. "Fewer clicks, more presence." Jobble Baby reduces parental cognitive load through ambient, glanceable tracking.

**CORE TRACKING — ONE TAP**
• Sleep tracker: nap cycles, nighttime sleep, wake windows
• Feeding log: breast, bottle, solids — duration, amount, side
• Diaper log: wet/dry/soiled with timestamp and notes
• Growth chart: WHO percentile for height, weight, head circumference

**MILESTONES + GEAR**
• Milestone tracker with photo capture + age stamp
• Allergen reaction timeline (FPIES/IgE/non-IgE)
• Sleep training tracker (Ferber/extinction/chair/fading)
• Context-aware outing checklist (weather + trip type)
• Doctor visit export — shareable pediatrician summary
• Milk prep guide: thaw guidelines, freezer stash, expiry

**PARENT wellbeing**
• Circadian dashboard + caregiver shift-handoff
• Parental burnout detection + breathing intervention
• Sleep debt dashboard — your deficit vs baby regression
• Emergency SOS: panic mode + safe space checklist

**WHY JOBBLE BABY?**
• Dark theme default + light mode (WCAG 2.1 AA)
• One hand, one tap — designed for 3am diaper changes
• All data on-device — no account, no cloud, no data selling
• Supports English and Traditional Chinese

**PRIVACY FIRST**
No account required. No analytics. No ads. All data stays on your device. Export/import JSON backup anytime.

Download Jobble Baby — fewer clicks, more presence.
