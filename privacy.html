1. Data Collection

Jobble Baby collects the following information to provide and improve our parenting support services:

  • Baby Profile Data: Name, date of birth, gender, and optional photo
  • Tracking Data: Feeding logs, diaper changes, sleep records, and growth measurements
  • Health Information: Allergen records, doctor visit notes, and milestone achievements
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Usage Analytics: Tab navigation and feature usage frequency (anonymous and aggregated only)
You are in control. All data is entered manually by you. We do not collect data from sensors, cameras, or microphones.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving app functionality
  • Sending optional reminders (scheduled alerts and milestone notifications)
  • Generating growth charts and milestone reports
  • Local storage on your device (no mandatory cloud sync)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or analytics companies.

4. Children's Privacy

Jobble Baby is a parenting tool that processes baby and child health data on behalf of parents and guardians. We comply with:

  • Apple's App Store Review Guidelines for children's apps
  • Google Play's Families Policy requirements
Important: We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age. Parents and guardians using the app are solely responsible for the accuracy of any data entered about a child.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. No mandatory cloud sync is required.
  • You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data from your device settings.
  • We implement reasonable security measures consistent with industry standards, but cannot guarantee 100% security.
  • Optional cloud backup (e.g., JSON export) is available in the app's Settings for manual backup by the user.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can request deletion or export your data at any time by:

  • Using the Export Data button in the app's Profile tab to download a full JSON backup
  • Clearing app data from your device's Settings menu
  • Contacting us at privacy@jobblebaby.com

7. Cookies

Jobble Baby does not use cookies or any similar tracking technologies. The app operates entirely offline with local storage on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Effective Date" above and, if we make material changes, we will notify you by posting a notice within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
