## Design Guidelines

- Dark theme background: `#0D0D0D`
- Accent color: `#3B82F6`
- All screenshots use app's dark theme for consistency
- Tab name displayed prominently in center
- Brief description below tab name
- "Jobble Baby" app name at bottom

## App Store Review Notes

Apple requires:
- At least 1 screenshot per app preview page
- Screenshots must accurately represent the app
- Each localized version may have different screenshots
- Minimum2 screenshots, maximum 10 per locale

Google Play requires:
- At least 2 screenshots
- First screenshot is the most important (featured in listing)
- Accepts up to 8 screenshots per store listing
